The first release of the zone quests pack, `MMOSkillQuestPack`: the Emerald Wilds campaign, Ranger Wren's Kweebec Nightmares arc and the Scarak Brood Queen world boss, with the Howling Sands campaign authored and shipped disabled. It is built on ZiggfreedCommon's quests, quest generators, achievements, conversations, NPC placements, lootables, feedback moments and encounter bindings, and on MMO Skill Tree's quest-giver template, reward kinds and stat channels, for Hytale Update 7. The pack ships no `Common/` folder: every model, texture and icon path it names is the game's own (the Onyxium armour and longsword, the Scarak egg sacks, each quest's icon).

- **Plugin id** `Ziggfreed:MMOSkillQuestPack` (manifest `Group` and `Name`, `IncludesAssetPack` true); `build.ps1` writes `MMOSkillQuestPack-1.0.0.zip` with forward-slash entries and an explicit directory entry for every ancestor path, carrying `manifest.json`, `icon-256.png` and `Server/`.
- **Requires** `ServerVersion` `>=0.7.0-pre.0 <0.8.0` (Update 7) and, under `Dependencies`, `Ziggfreed:MMOSkillTree` `^1.7.1` and `Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon` `>=2.3.0`. MMO Skill Tree is hard: the three giver roles are Variants of its `Template_Mmo_QuestGiver`, Word from the Wilds is offered by its `Mmo_Hub` and requires its `gather_the_basics`, the rewards pay its `Mmo_Xp`, `Mmo_Lamp` and `Mmo_Boost_Token` kinds, the level gates read its `MMO_TotalLevel` and `MMO_Level_<SKILL>` stats, and the gear writes its `MMO_*` stat channels. ZiggfreedCommon is hard: every quest, generator, achievement, conversation, placement, lootable, feedback moment and the encounter binding is one of its asset types, and the boss script runs its `ZigRested` sensor and its encounter macros.
- **Optional** `Ziggfreed:KweebecNightmare` `*` (manifest `OptionalDependencies`): the Kweebec arc's five quests and the three Night Owl achievements gate on `hytale:mod_installed` `Ziggfreed:KweebecNightmare` with `Min` 1 and count its rounds through `INSTANCE_ROUND_WON` targets, so on a server without it they are never offered or listed. The pack reaches the mod only through that factor and the `kweebec:` targets, and names none of its assets.
- **Ids and folders.** Quests sit under `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Quests/MMOSkillTree/Zones/`, where the `_Wilds` and `_Sands` folders prefix every id beneath them (`_Wilds/Meet_The_Guide.json` is `wilds_meet_the_guide`), so renaming either folder renames every quest under it and starts anyone mid-campaign over. The two bases sit in the unmarked `Zones` folder (`zone_gather_base`, `zone_slay_base`), and a generated quest takes no prefix: each generator row spells its full `wilds_` or `sands_` id. Achievements sit under `Achievements/MMOSkillTree/Zones/`, conversations under `Dialogues/MMOSkillTree/`, the pack's lootables carry an `Mmo_` prefix, and the boss's role, marker, prefab and clutch files sit in `MMOSkillQuestPack` subfolders. `Server/MMOSkillTree/Control/MMOSkillQuestPack.json` lists no store, since the pack ships nothing into a store MMO Skill Tree owns.
- **Shared bases and generators.** `Zone_Gather_Base` and `Zone_Slay_Base` are Abstract: `Listing` `Category` `main` with `Hidden` and `RequirePrerequisites` true, `Tool_Pickaxe_Crude` and `Weapon_Sword_Crude` as their `Listing.Icon`, and one `main` step (`BREAK_BLOCK`, or `KILL_ENTITY` with `MatchMode` `CONTAINS`). The QuestGenerators `Zone_Gather` (`Base` `zone_gather_base`) and `Zone_Slay` (`Base` `zone_slay_base`) write one quest per `ForEach` row under `IdPattern` `{quest}`: `wilds_tools_of_the_trade`, `sands_quarry_of_kings` and `sands_hardwood_of_the_dunes` (a bulk `main` step plus a second `BREAK_BLOCK` step the row names, such as `copper` or `palm`), and `wilds_trork_trouble`, `wilds_graveyard_watch` and `sands_hive_incursion`. A row that leaves `gift` or `xp2` empty writes a reward row with an empty `Kind`, which pays nothing.

## World and NPCs

- Givers: three roles under `Server/NPC/Roles/Passive/`, each a `Variant` of `Template_Mmo_QuestGiver` (which passes through to ZiggfreedCommon's `Template_Zig_QuestGiver`, holding the body and the press-F action) that sets only `Appearance` and `NameTranslationKey`: `Guide_Wilds` (Ranger Wren, `Feran_Windwalker`), `Quartermaster_Wilds` (Quartermaster Bramble, `Klops_Merchant`) and `Guide_Sands` (Dunewalker Ashkar, `Outlander_Hunter`). Their press-F prompt is ZiggfreedCommon's `npcs.Zig_QuestGiver.hint`.
- Placements (`Server/ZiggfreedCommon/NpcPlacements/`): `Guide_Wilds`, `Quartermaster_Wilds` and `Guide_Sands`, each `Where` `Match` `["default"]` with an `Anchor.Structure` on a worldgen marker (`MarkerIds` `Kweebec`, `Kweebec_Merchant` and `Outlander_Hunter`, `Yaw` 180), `Limits` `SpawnChance` 1.0 and `MaxPerWorld` 1, and `Interact.Dialogue` naming the giver's conversation. `MarkerIds` is a fail-closed allow-list, so a marker id the world never loads leaves that giver absent. None authors an `Identity.NpcId`, so the role id is the character id the quests' `Npc.ViewId`, `TurnInId`, `TurnInNpcId` and talk steps name.
- Conversations (`Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Dialogues/MMOSkillTree/`): `Guide_Wilds_Dialogue` keeps `Start.Quests` rows for the two Kweebec story quests (`nightmare_offer`, `nightmare_brief` and `nightmare_ready`; `nightmare_deeper`, `nightmare_deeper_brief` and `nightmare_deeper_ready`, whose hand-in opens `nightmare_vigil`, the line that points at the dailies), `Then` greetings on `wilds_call_of_the_dunes` and `wilds_meet_the_guide` `COMPLETED`, and a `Fallback` `greet` whose intro option, while `wilds_meet_the_guide` is `ACTIVE`, runs `MarkTalked`, hands it in and opens `camp_talk`, which carries the `Once` bread option (an `ITEM` reward of three `Food_Bread`). `Quartermaster_Wilds_Dialogue` greets a player mid-ledger with the hand-in (`greet_active`, a `TurnIn` of `wilds_quartermasters_ledger`), and its trade option runs `MarkTalked`. `Guide_Sands_Dialogue` opens `greet_known` once `sands_walker_of_the_wastes` is `COMPLETED`, shows its intro option (`MarkTalked`, a `TurnIn` of `wilds_call_of_the_dunes`) while that quest is `ACTIVE`, and the two Brood Queen lines (`brood_queen`, `brood_queen_where`) once `sands_apex_of_the_sands` is `COMPLETED`; with the desert arc held, his conversation reaches only `greet`, whose one visible option (`greet.opt.work`, shown always) runs `MarkTalked`, so Well Met's `Guide_Sands` criterion and a talk step at him credit there too, and opens his quest list.

## Quests

- Emerald Wilds, 11 quests. The front door `wilds_meet_the_guide` (Word from the Wilds) has `Npc.ViewId` `Mmo_Hub` and `TurnInId` `Guide_Wilds`, requires `gather_the_basics`, and is one blank-target `TURN_IN` at Wren. Wren (`Guide_Wilds`) gives `wilds_first_camp`, `wilds_hearty_meal`, `wilds_trork_trouble`, `wilds_graveyard_watch` (`MMO_TotalLevel` 10), `wilds_proving_day` (after the Graveyard Watch and the Ledger, `MMO_TotalLevel` 20; a `GAIN_XP` `TOTAL` 10000 step and a `REACH_LEVEL` 15 step whose blank `Target` means any one skill) and `wilds_call_of_the_dunes` (`Npc.TurnInId` `Guide_Sands`, a blank-target `TURN_IN` at Ashkar). Bramble (`Quartermaster_Wilds`) gives `wilds_tools_of_the_trade`, `wilds_line_and_lure`, `wilds_quartermasters_ledger` (a `TURN_IN` of 10 `Ingredient_Hide_Light`, `EXACT`, `TurnInNpcId` `giver`) and `wilds_friend_of_beasts` (gated on `mmoskilltree:feature` `taming` `Min` 1; `FEED_ANIMAL` 3 and `BREED_ANIMAL` 1).
- Kweebec Nightmares (`Listing.Tags` `Nightmare`, all five gated on `hytale:mod_installed`): `wilds_whispers_after_dark` (after Proving Day; one `INSTANCE_ROUND_WON` with `Target` `kweebec:`, `MatchMode` `PREFIX` and no `Qualifier`) and `wilds_face_the_nightmare` (`Qualifier` `Nightmare`), both `TurnInAt` `giver`; then the dailies `wilds_amateur_night`, `wilds_nightmare_shift` and `wilds_hardcore_vigil` (`Qualifier` `Amateur`, `Nightmare` and `Hardcore`), each after Face the Nightmare with `Repeat.Reset` `Period` `Daily` and `Listing.Chains` `wilds_nightmare_daily` tiers 1 to 3. The dailies name no `TurnInAt` and pay through `Rewards.Claim`, so the pay waits in the quest log for Collect wherever the player stands; each rolls its own lootable (`Mmo_Kweebec_Daily_Amateur`, `_Nightmare` or `_Hardcore`) beside flat DEFENSE experience.
- The Howling Sands campaign is held. The six `_Sands` files (`sands_walker_of_the_wastes`, `sands_thorns_and_stings`, `sands_oasis_angler`, `sands_bones_of_the_old_kingdom`, `sands_apex_of_the_sands` and `sands_brood_below`) carry `Enabled: false`; the three generated contracts (`sands_quarry_of_kings`, `sands_hardwood_of_the_dunes`, `sands_hive_incursion`) carry no `Enabled` leaf but require a disabled quest (`sands_walker_of_the_wastes`, or `sands_thorns_and_stings` for the hive), so none can start. The front door `sands_walker_of_the_wastes` is `Flow.AutoAccept` after `wilds_call_of_the_dunes`, with one `TALK_TO_NPC` step at `Guide_Sands`. The campaign gates read `MMO_TotalLevel` (40, 60, 80 and 100) and the trade quests their own skill (`MMO_Level_MINING`, `MMO_Level_WOODCUTTING`, `MMO_Level_FISHING`, each 15); `sands_bones_of_the_old_kingdom` takes an `AnyOf` of the three trade quests. Setting `Enabled` back on the six quests and the two held achievements opens the arc.
- The Brood Below (`sands_brood_below`, `Listing.Tags` `Boss`, after `sands_apex_of_the_sands`, `TurnInAt` `giver`): one `ENCOUNTER_DEFEATED` step, worded by `objective.text.quest.brood_queen`, whose `Target` is the encounter script id `Sands_Brood_Queen_Encounter` (`EXACT`) rather than the queen's role, since the fight swaps her role between phases. It pays `Mmo_Xp` `ALL` 3000 and 5 `Potion_Health_Greater`.
- Every quest names a vanilla item as its `Listing.Icon`, and the generated contracts inherit their base's; no quest carries a cadence tag of its own.

## Achievements

- `emerald_wilds_campaign` (Warden of the Wilds, `COMPLETE_QUEST` `wilds_call_of_the_dunes`, 50 points); `well_met` (three `TALK_TO_NPC` criteria, one per giver, 15 points, no reward); `wilds_hunter_t1` and `_t2` (Wilds Stalker and Wilds Apex: `KILL_ENTITY` with a blank `Target`, `CONTAINS` and `Zone` `Emerald_Wilds`, to 50 and 500) and `sands_hunter_t1` and `_t2` (Dune Stalker and Dune Apex, `Zone` `Howling_Sands`, 50 and 500), each pair a `Listing.Chains` ladder sharing one description key filled from `@amount`.
- The Night Owl ladder `night_owl_t1` to `_t3` (Night Owl, Grove Watcher and Nightwarden; chain `night_owl`; gated on `hytale:mod_installed`): `INSTANCE_ROUND_WON` `kweebec:` `PREFIX` with no `Qualifier`, to 7, 21 and 50, paying `Nightwarden_Hood`, `Nightwarden_Cuirass` and `Nightwarden_Greaves` in that order.
- Held with the desert arc, both `Enabled: false`: `howling_sands_campaign` (Crown of the Dunes, `COMPLETE_QUEST` `sands_apex_of_the_sands`) and `orbis_campaigner` (Campaigner of Orbis: `MetaChildren` both campaign achievements, `Meta.mmoskilltree` `ServerFirst` true with an `Announce`).

## The Brood Queen

- Encounter script `Server/EncounterManager/Sands_Brood_Queen_Encounter.json` (`Class` `EncounterManager`, `StartState` `Intro`, `CleanupOnRemove` true), shaped on ZiggfreedCommon's `Zc_Encounter_Base`. A `Player` sensor at 80 blocks with the `EncounterMembers` collector keeps the roster. In `Intro`, `TriggerSpawners` raises the queen from `Sands_Brood_Queen_Marker` into the `Boss` slot while no boss is bound and `ZigRested` passes; on bind the script runs `SetTargetNPCInvulnerable` false and `SetEncounterBossBar` `npcs.Sands_Brood_Queen.name`, signals `zc:engaged` and enters `Phase_1`, and a rested site with no queen bound after 30 seconds signals `zc:mmoquestpack:no_show` and retires to `Complete`. `Zc_Phase_At_Health` (`HealthRange` 0 to 0.5, `SwapRole`) moves her to `Sands_Brood_Queen_Phase2` and then `_Phase3` (signals `zc:phase:Phase_2` and `zc:phase:Phase_3`), `Zc_Adds_Wave` fires the `Sands_Brood_Marker` clutches (3 in `Phase_2`, 4 in `Phase_3`), and `Zc_Defeat_Beat` sits in every phase and in `Standing`. No player within 80 blocks for 60 seconds clears the bar and moves to `Standing`, where the next player near sends `zc:reset` and re-engages her at the health she was left with. `Complete` waits 300 seconds, sends `zc:reset` and returns to `Intro`, where `ZigRested` holds the next summon until the rest has passed.
- Roles `Server/NPC/Roles/MMOSkillQuestPack/Sands_Brood_Queen.json`, `_Phase2` and `_Phase3`: Variants of `Template_Scarak_Broodmother` with `MaxHealth` 1200, 600 and 300, `_CombatConfig.EntityEffects` `Immunity_Environmental` and `Immunity_Poison`, and one `NameTranslationKey`, `npcs.Sands_Brood_Queen.name`. Markers `Sands_Brood_Queen_Marker` (the queen) and `Sands_Brood_Marker` (`Scarak_Fighter` weight 60, `Scarak_Louse` 40), both `ManualTrigger` true with `DeactivationDistance` 512 and `DeactivationTime` 150.
- Arena: the blocks `Sands_Brood_Queen_Spawner` (Brood Queen's Clutch) and `Sands_Brood_Spawner` (Scarak Brood Clutch) under `Server/Item/Items/MMOSkillQuestPack/`, the game's Scarak egg sack at `CustomModelScale` 1.4 and 0.8, each with a `SpawnMarkerBlock` (`MarkerOffset` Y 1 and Y 2) and no `Gathering` group or `Support` rule; and the prefab `Server/Prefabs/MMOSkillQuestPack/Sands_Brood_Queen_Spawners.prefab.json`, the queen's clutch at its centre and five brood clutches around it, each block entry writing its own marker config. An owner pastes it once per world (`/prefab load MMOSkillQuestPack/Sands_Brood_Queen_Spawners`) and stands the script up on it with `/zigencounter spawn Sands_Brood_Queen_Encounter`; the encounter entity saves with the world.
- Binding row `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Encounters/Sands_Brood_Queen_Encounter.json`: `NameKey` `npcs.Sands_Brood_Queen.name`, `Subject.TargetSlot` `Boss`, `Scale` `HealthPerMember` 0.3 and `MaxHealthMultiplier` 4.0, `Timing` `WipeGraceSeconds` 45 and `Rest` `P1D`, `Loot.OnDefeat` the lootable `Mmo_Sands_Brood_Queen` with `QueueIfOffline` true, `Leaderboard` bucket `sands_brood_queen` with `ByPartySize` true, `Feedback` naming the four moments below, and `Discovery` (`MapMarker` true, `MarkerIcon` `PortalInvasion.png`, `FollowSeconds` 3, `CompassUpdating` true). Participation is left to ZiggfreedCommon's `Zc_Default` rule. The rest is stamped on the encounter entity at the kill and saved with the world, so it survives a restart; an owner turns `Timing.Rest`, or switches the fight off with `Enabled` false, under `sands_brood_queen_encounter` in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/encounters.json`, and `/zigencounter reload` re-reads it.
- `Mmo_Sands_Brood_Queen` is rolled once per credited participant, each roll kept at that participant's share: a first roll that always pays 10 `Ingredient_Life_Essence` and 2 `Potion_Health_Greater`, a 60 percent roll for 1 to 2 `Ingredient_Bar_Thorium`, and a 20 percent roll for an `Mmo_Boost_Token` (`ALL`, x2.0, 30 minutes).
- Feedback moments (`Server/ZiggfreedCommon/FeedbackMoments/`): `Sands_Brood_Queen_Engaged`, a `Major` `Broadcast` with `ToParticipants`, `SameWorldOnly` and `MinSecondsBetween` 60; `Sands_Brood_Queen_Phase_Changed` and `Sands_Brood_Queen_Wiped`, `Info` toasts; and `Sands_Brood_Queen_Defeated`, a `Reward` toast whose second line is ZiggfreedCommon's `ziggfreedcommon.feedback.encounter.defeated.share` (args `share` and `seconds`), with the sound `SFX_Discovery_Z2_Short`.

- The Nightwarden set, each `Quality` Epic and `ItemLevel` 40 on a cobalt `Parent` with the Onyxium `Icon`, `Model` and `Texture`: `Nightwarden_Longsword` (`Weapon_Longsword_Cobalt`; `Weapon.StatModifiers` `MMO_Bonus_Void` 8, `MMO_BonusFlat_Void` 2, `MMO_Damage_SWORDS` 5 and `Mana` 2; `Tags.MMO_Weapon_Type` `SWORDS`), and `Nightwarden_Hood`, `Nightwarden_Cuirass` and `Nightwarden_Greaves` (`Armor_Cobalt_Head`, `_Chest` and `_Legs`), each restating the parent's whole `Armor` block and adding `MMO_SchoolResist_Void` 6, `MMO_Bonus_Void` 2 and `Mana` 1, with one Additive `Health` entry (17, 32 and 22) and, on the Cuirass alone, `Stamina` 2.

## Rewards

- Every reward row in the pack's quests and achievements sits in `Rewards.Claim`, collected by the player; the pack has no `Rewards.Auto` row.
- `Mmo_Lamp`, MMO Skill Tree's Skill Lamp: the every-skill lamp (`Picks` 3, no `Skills`) at `Amount` 7500 on `emerald_wilds_campaign`, and the combat lamp (`Picks` 1, `Skills` `COMBAT`) on `wilds_trork_trouble` 800 and `wilds_graveyard_watch` 700 (written by `Zone_Slay`'s `Child` from each row's `xp1_amount`), `wilds_face_the_nightmare` 1500, `wilds_hunter_t2` 2500 and `sands_hunter_t2` 5000. Held with the desert arc: the every-skill lamp at 15000 on `howling_sands_campaign`, and combat lamps on `sands_thorns_and_stings` 1200, `sands_hive_incursion` 2500 and `sands_bones_of_the_old_kingdom` 3000.
- `Mmo_Xp`, in the skill the work trains: `wilds_first_camp` WOODCUTTING and BUILDING 400, `wilds_hearty_meal` DEFENSE 500, `wilds_tools_of_the_trade` MINING 600, `wilds_line_and_lure` FISHING 700, `wilds_quartermasters_ledger` DEFENSE and HARVESTING 600, `wilds_friend_of_beasts` TAMING 800, `wilds_graveyard_watch` DEFENSE 700, `wilds_call_of_the_dunes` ACROBATICS 1000, `wilds_whispers_after_dark` DEFENSE and ACROBATICS 800, `wilds_face_the_nightmare` DEFENSE 1500, and the dailies DEFENSE 500, 1000 and 1500.
- `Mmo_Boost_Token`: `wilds_proving_day` (`ALL`, x2.0, 30 minutes), and inside lootables `Mmo_Kweebec_Daily_Nightmare` (`ALL`, x1.5, 20 minutes), `Mmo_Kweebec_Daily_Hardcore` (`ALL`, x2.0, 30 minutes) and the queen's third roll. The daily tables pay `Ingredient_Life_Essence` (3, 6, and 4 beside 2 `Ingredient_Life_Essence_Concentrated`).
- Items: Word from the Wilds pays 3 `Food_Bread`, a `Bench_Campfire` and the Prospecting Pick, Tools of the Trade a `Tool_Pickaxe_Iron`, Trork Trouble a `Weapon_Longsword_Iron`, Face the Nightmare the Nightwarden Longsword, and the Night Owl rungs the three armour pieces.

## Lang

- `Server/Languages/<locale>/mmoskilltree.lang` (127 bare keys in the `mmoskilltree` namespace: quest titles and flavors, objective lines, the three conversations, the queen's notices `boss.sands_brood_queen.*` and the achievements), `npcs.lang` (the three givers' names, `Sands_Brood_Queen.name`, and `Mmo_QuestGiver.hint` for a giver whose own role body names it) and `server.lang` (the seven item names, `items.<Id>.name`). All three ship in nine locales (de-DE, en-US, es-ES, fr-FR, hu-HU, it-IT, pt-BR, ru-RU and tr-TR), each file carrying the same keys as en-US.
