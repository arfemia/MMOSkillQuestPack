# MMOSkillQuestPack 1.1.0

Ships alongside MMO Skill Tree [[1.6.1]] and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.1.0, which the pack
requires from this version on.

- **New: the Scarak Brood Queen, a world boss for the Howling Sands.** A brood queen the size of
  a wagon, run by the game's own encounter scripts, with three phases and her brood coming up
  from the sand as she weakens. Everyone who fights her is credited for the fight, not just the
  last hit: the loot she pays is rolled for each participant and scaled by how much of the
  fight was theirs, a boss bar tracks her under her own name, a world-map marker follows her
  while she stands, the server hears she is up, and the fight writes its own leaderboard. She
  comes back on her own after a kill, one in-game day later.
- **New: you place her, once.** The pack ships the fight and the arena; a server owner picks
  the spot. In creative with builder tools, `/prefab load MMOSkillQuestPack/Sands_Brood_Queen_Spawners`
  puts the arena (her clutch and five brood clutches) in your clipboard; place it where the
  fight belongs, stand on it and run `/zigencounter spawn Sands_Brood_Queen_Encounter`. That is
  the whole install, once per world, ever: the fight is saved with the world. Switch her off
  from `mods/ziggfreedcommon/encounters.json` if you change your mind.
- **New: The Brood Below**, the quest that caps the Howling Sands campaign, from Dunewalker
  Ashkar once the apex hunts are done, with two new lines of his about what waits under the old
  kingdom. It ships held with the rest of the desert campaign (see the 1.0.0 notes), so it
  opens when that campaign does; the fight itself is live from the moment you place it.
- **Fixed:** an authoring line on Ranger Wren's bread offer tripped the content audit with a
  warning; the offer worked before and works the same now, the warning is gone.
- Every new quest line, dialogue line, boss notice and item name ships in all 9 languages.
