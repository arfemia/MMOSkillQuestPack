First release, built for Hytale's Update 7. It adds a questline in the Emerald Wilds, a Kweebec Nightmares arc for servers that run that minigame, and the Scarak Brood Queen, a world boss a server owner places once. It needs an Update 7 server, MMO Skill Tree [[1.7.1]] and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer, and it won't load on Update 6.

### New quests

- **Added the Emerald Wilds campaign, 11 quests.** The Adventurer's Guide at the hub hands you "Word from the Wilds" and sends you to Ranger Wren's camp beside a Kweebec village. From there Wren and Quartermaster Bramble give you quests such as First Camp, A Hearty Meal, The Quartermaster's Ledger, Trork Trouble, Graveyard Watch and Proving Day, and the campaign ends with The Call of the Dunes. The first quest waits until you've finished MMO Skill Tree's intro tutorial, so the campaign starts once you know the basics.
- **Added the Kweebec Nightmares arc, 2 quests and 3 dailies.** Once Proving Day is behind you, Ranger Wren asks you to win a round of Kweebec Nightmare, then to win one on the Nightmare preset. After that she offers three dailies: Amateur Night, Nightmare Shift and Hardcore Vigil, each a round won on that preset. For the dailies, the pay waits in your quest log once you win: press Collect there, wherever you are, with no trip back to Wren. The arc only shows up on a server that also runs Kweebec Nightmare.
- **Added conversations for the quest givers.** Ranger Wren and Quartermaster Bramble greet you differently as the campaign goes on. Wren tells you about the wilds if you ask, has lines for each step of the nightmare arc and hands you three loaves of bread once. You hand in Word from the Wilds by talking to Wren, and The Quartermaster's Ledger by talking to Bramble.
- **Added a picture for every quest.** It shows beside the quest wherever it is listed and on the notice when you finish it, such as a campfire, a cooked meal, a fish or a collar in the Emerald Wilds, and a Kweebec item for each of the five nightmare quests. Repeating quests show a Daily, Weekly or Repeatable badge in every quest list.

### A world boss

- **Added the Scarak Brood Queen.** A Scarak queen of the Howling Sands with three phases, and more of her brood joins the fight as she weakens. Everyone who fights her gets credit for the fight, not just the last hit: her loot is rolled for each of you and scaled by how much of the fight was yours. A boss bar shows her name, a marker follows her on the world map while she stands, everyone in her world hears when she is up, and her fight gets its own tab under Boss Fights on the Records tab. After a kill she rests one game day, even across a restart, then rises again from the same clutch when the next party comes near.
- **A server owner places her, once.** The pack ships the fight and the arena, and you pick the spot. In creative with builder tools, `/prefab load MMOSkillQuestPack/Sands_Brood_Queen_Spawners` puts the arena (her clutch and five brood clutches) in your clipboard. Place it where the fight belongs (in creative a marker figure stands over each clutch as soon as it lands; adventure players never see them), stand on it and run `/zigencounter spawn Sands_Brood_Queen_Encounter`. That is the whole install, once per world: the fight is saved with the world. Fight her in adventure, since a creative player doesn't count as part of the fight. To switch her off, or change how long she rests (`Timing.Rest`), edit `mods/ziggfreedcommon/encounters.json`.

### Achievements and rewards

- **Added achievements for both zones.** The Emerald Wilds campaign capstone, Well Met for talking with all three quest givers, hunter ladders for the Emerald Wilds and the Howling Sands, and the three-rung Night Owl ladder for Kweebec Nightmare rounds won, each rung giving you a piece of the Nightwarden set. The Howling Sands capstone and the Campaigner of Orbis achievement wait with the rest of that campaign (see below).
- **Some rewards are Skill Lamps.** Warden of the Wilds gives a Skill Lamp: rub it and choose three skills, and each one gets the lamp's experience. Trork Trouble, Graveyard Watch, Face the Nightmare, Wilds Apex and Dune Apex give a lamp for one combat skill of your choice.

### New items

- **Added Wren's Prospecting Pick.** It adds Mining luck and Mining experience, and Wren hands it to you the first time you reach her camp.
- **Added the Nightwarden set.** A Longsword (Void damage, Swords damage and a little mana) and a Hood, Cuirass and Greaves (Void resistance, Void damage, extra health and a little mana; the Cuirass also adds a little stamina), all in the game's dark Onyxium look. You earn every piece, and none of them can be crafted.

### Languages

- **Fully translated.** Every quest, conversation line, achievement, boss notice and item name is in all 9 languages.

### Held back for a later release

The Howling Sands campaign (8 quests, Walker of the Wastes through Apex of the Sands), The Brood Below (the quest that caps it, from Dunewalker Ashkar once Apex of the Sands is done) and the two achievements for finishing it, the Howling Sands capstone and Campaigner of Orbis, are written but switched off in 1.0.0, so the first release stays small and well tested. The Emerald Wilds campaign still ends cleanly with The Call of the Dunes, which you hand in to Dunewalker Ashkar, but he has nothing more for you yet. The Brood Queen herself is live as soon as she's placed; only the quest about her waits.
