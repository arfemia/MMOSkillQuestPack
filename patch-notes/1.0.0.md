# MMOSkillQuestPack 1.0.0

First release, alongside MMO Skill Tree [[1.6.1]] and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.1.0, which the
pack requires.

- **Emerald Wilds campaign** (11 quests): the Adventurer's Guide at the hub hands out
  "Word from the Wilds" and points you east to Ranger Wren's camp beside the kweebec
  village; raise your first camp, hunt your supper, run Quartermaster Bramble's supply
  ledger, push back the trorks, stand the graveyard watch, prove yourself on Proving Day
  and earn the road south. The opening quest waits until you've finished MMO Skill Tree's
  own intro tutorial, so the campaign only picks up once you know the basics.
- **Kweebec Nightmares arc** (2 quests plus 3 dailies): once Proving Day is behind you,
  Ranger Wren starts hearing screams out of the kweebec grove after dark. Survive one
  round of Kweebec Nightmare to bring her the news, then face the blighted thing at its
  worst, and after that she keeps three standing contracts going: a gentle night, a bad
  one, and a worse one, each resetting daily and paying out the moment you win. The whole
  arc only appears on a server that also runs the Kweebec Nightmare minigame, and stays
  completely out of sight on one that does not.
- **The Scarak Brood Queen, a world boss for the Howling Sands.** A brood queen the size of
  a wagon, run by the game's own encounter scripts, with three phases and her brood coming up
  from the sand as she weakens. Everyone who fights her is credited for the fight, not just the
  last hit: the loot she pays is rolled for each participant and scaled by how much of the
  fight was theirs, a boss bar tracks her under her own name, a world-map marker follows her
  while she stands, the server hears she is up, and the fight writes its own leaderboard. She
  rests one game day after a kill, a rest the library keeps on the fight itself so it survives a
  restart, and rises again from the same clutch for the next party that comes near.
- **You place her, once.** The pack ships the fight and the arena; a server owner picks
  the spot. In creative with builder tools, `/prefab load MMOSkillQuestPack/Sands_Brood_Queen_Spawners`
  puts the arena (her clutch and five brood clutches) in your clipboard; place it where the
  fight belongs (in creative a spawn-marker figure stands over each clutch as soon as it lands;
  adventure players never see them), stand on it and run
  `/zigencounter spawn Sands_Brood_Queen_Encounter`. That is the whole install, once per world,
  ever: the fight is saved with the world. Then fight her in adventure: a creative player is not
  counted as part of the fight. Switch her off from `mods/ziggfreedcommon/encounters.json` if you
  change your mind, and tune how long she rests there too (`Timing.Rest`).
- **Branching NPC dialogue** for all three givers: progress-aware greetings, lore
  branches, a one-time bread handout, Wren's night-arc beats, intro options that
  drive the talk objectives, and two lines of Dunewalker Ashkar's about what waits under
  the old kingdom.
- **Achievements**: the Emerald Wilds campaign capstone, Well Met, zone-scoped hunter
  chains for both zones, and the three-rung Night Owl ladder counting Kweebec Nightmare
  rounds won, each rung handing over a piece of the Nightwarden set. The Howling Sands
  capstone and the Campaigner of Orbis meta are held back with the rest of that campaign,
  see below.
- **Earned-only gear**: Wren's Prospecting Pick (Mining luck and Mining experience, handed
  over the first time you reach her camp), and the Nightwarden set: a Longsword (Void
  damage and Swords damage) plus a Hood, Cuirass and Greaves (Void resistance, Void
  damage, extra health, and a taste of mana - the Cuirass also adds a little stamina),
  all reskinned onto the game's dark Onyxium look instead of the plain cobalt they're
  built from. None of them are craftable; all of them are earned.
- **Every quest has a picture.** All twenty-one name one, drawn beside the quest wherever it is
  listed and on the notice when it finishes: the sands read as venom, bone, sandstone and a desert
  torch, the wilds as a campfire, a cooked meal, a fish and a collar, and the five nightmare shifts
  as Kweebec things. A quest of your own that inherits from one of the pack's two bases starts with
  a pickaxe or a sword and can name its own. The repeating quests carry no cadence label of their
  own; every quest list badges them Daily, Weekly or Repeatable off their timing.
- **Fully translated**: every quest, dialogue line, achievement, boss notice and item name ships
  in all 9 languages.

## Held back for a later release

The Howling Sands campaign (8 quests: Walker of the Wastes through Apex of the Sands), the quest
that caps it (The Brood Below, from Dunewalker Ashkar once the apex hunts are done) and the two
achievements gated on finishing it, the Howling Sands capstone and the Campaigner of Orbis meta,
are authored and complete but ship disabled in 1.0.0, so the first release stays a tighter,
fully-tested slice. Emerald Wilds still hands off cleanly at its finale, Call of the Dunes: you'll
meet Dunewalker Ashkar out in the desert, he just has nothing further to offer yet. The Brood
Queen herself is live from the moment you place her; only the quest about her waits. Nothing
about the campaign needs re-authoring to turn it back on.
